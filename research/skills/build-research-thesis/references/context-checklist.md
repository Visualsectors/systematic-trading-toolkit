# Context in a thesis

Read Price, Peers and Market separately. Cite exact lane-owned evidence IDs. Challenger sentiment/coverage momentum, measured SPY reaction and stock-theme linkage qualify a narrative; headlines do not establish causation. A rising challenger adverse to the thesis becomes a stated falsifier/review condition.

Compare SPY correlation, beta and direction only on aligned supplied sessions. Without an earlier frozen context, describe current co-movement, not a flip. Prefer equal-weight peer context when capitalization is dominated; preserve cap-weight differences. Headline-peer co-mentions and business-line splits can contradict industry-only readings, but do not pick new peers.

Mark all missing peer membership, capitalization, breadth and news linkage fields “not supplied.” Partial live coverage cannot support a full point-in-time claim or complete peer assessment. Check differing source cutoffs between snapshot research and context; do not merge them into a falsely synchronized view.
