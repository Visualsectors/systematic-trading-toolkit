# Limitations and responsible use

- The bundled symbols, observations, news, and company facts are fictional.
- A screen narrows a research universe; it does not estimate expected return or suitability.
- Support and resistance may fail, gap, disappear, or be calculated from erroneous data.
- ATR-width zones and invalidation levels are conventions, not guarantees of execution or loss containment.
- Stop-risk sizing can materially understate realized loss when price gaps through a stop.
- Volatility estimates are backward-looking and depend on provider definitions and adjustment choices.
- The research brief is bounded by supplied evidence. Missing evidence is a gap, not a neutral signal.
- Risk flags are incomplete by construction and do not cover every market, liquidity, operational, legal, tax, concentration, leverage, short-sale, or counterparty risk.
- Monitoring detects changes in supplied risk flags. It cannot detect what the provider does not supply.
- No broker integration or automated order placement exists.
- No included preset has been presented as a profitable strategy or validated through a backtest.
- AI presentation layers may hallucinate. Treat structured toolkit output and cited sources as the audit record, and require human review before a consequential decision.

Production adoption requires independent validation, licensed point-in-time data, access control, observability, incident handling, and legal/compliance review appropriate to the operator and jurisdiction.
