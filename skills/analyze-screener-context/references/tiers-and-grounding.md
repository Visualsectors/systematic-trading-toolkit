# Tiers and grounding

Use the exact request's immutable tier rules, admitted interpretations, eligible
codes and numeric fact index. The saved dispatched request is bound to the exact
screen input, prompt, schema, evidence and field/value-specific atoms. Model
draft prose is never the displayed card: code validates and renders it.

- **A:** strengthened thesis, complete critical lanes, a real current Price
  tailwind and an eligible current-context positive, with no material risk,
  headwind, mixed or insufficient lane. Historical screen priors and proxy-news
  support cannot create A. A can be empty.
- **B:** mixed thesis fit; evaluable evidence is credible but conflicted, thin,
  extended or needs a specific confirmation. Partial evidence cannot support A.
- **C:** contradicted thesis fit and at least one contextual headwind. Missing
  evidence alone is not contradiction.
- **INSUFFICIENT_DATA:** a critical lane is insufficient, thesis fit is unknown,
  and matching missing codes are visible. This is abstention, not bearishness.

Copy required risks and make them visible in the appropriate lane and cited
headwind. Cite the candidate's own Price summary/tape and available gap/pattern
rows; peer summary, scoped news, weighting/headline peers when supplied; Market
summary, both benchmark comparisons, co-movement and the supplied narrative
roles. Watch a challenger adverse to the screen side. Never swap 10-session and
21-session facts or type model-authored numbers into prose.

Custom screens have no performance prior until a separate reviewed methodology
exists. First-party presets may ground only their declared primary horizon when
it has matured. Calendar 1W/1M/1Q outcomes are not fixed 5/21/63-session returns.
Keep the exact filter IDs, screen side and order; never infer a first-party
descriptor from its display name. Retain contrary evidence and coverage gaps.

The CLI constructs a request but never sends it. Model calls require the user's
chosen service and authorization. Store inputs, request and raw response locally
under their data rights; do not commit licensed rows or credentials. Keep the
retrieval and release hashes for replay. A parser refusal does not authorize
changing policy, retries with paid models, or publication of an unvalidated card.
